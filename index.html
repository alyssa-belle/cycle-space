A helpful guide for space users

A good visit, from request to review.

Know what to expect before you arrive, how to care for a space, and how to share feedback afterward.

Prototype note: These steps describe how a real booking would work. This demo does not confirm bookings, provide access details, or accept ratings and reviews.

01

Before you go

Request a time and wait for confirmation.

Check the listing’s location, rate, capacity, and details. Share what you plan to do, then wait for the owner to confirm your booking before making the trip.

⌖

Getting there

Use the directions in your booking details.

After confirmation, Cycle Space will provide the full address, directions, arrival and entry instructions, and a way to contact our team in your booking details. Arrive during your booked time and keep any access codes private.

↗

While you’re there

Leave the space as you found it.

Follow the instructions Cycle Space provides and use only the areas agreed for your booking. You’re responsible for damage caused by you or your group beyond normal wear and tear; tell Cycle Space promptly if anything is damaged. If you spot existing damage, let us know before use.

★

After your visit

Rate your experience honestly.

After a completed booking, leave a rating and a helpful review about the space and your experience. Specific, respectful feedback helps future users know what to expect and helps owners improve.

?

Need help during a visit?

Contact Cycle Space for help. Through our relationship with the building owner, we may be able to help resolve the issue. For an urgent safety concern, leave the space and contact local emergency services.
