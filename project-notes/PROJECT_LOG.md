## Reflection (fill in at the end)

1. What surprised you most about building software with AI? 
The most surprising thing to me was how quickly an AI chat could turn a prompt into something real. I have no previous coding experience, so getting to see all the behind the scenes operations and writing that comes together to create a website is interesting. I expected building a website to require much more technical knowledge and skill, but Ai makes it possible to focus more on the thinking part and let it do the harder work for you. Building a website is way different than creating a business but having this knowledge and platform help in creating a real prototype was really interesting. 
2. Which parts were easy, and which were hard? Why do you think that is?
The easiest part was coming up with a business idea and explaining it to the AI chat. It was very simple to create new ideas, imagery, pages, and information sections. Updating the website was so simple even when AI made some mistakes and did not understand the prompt exactly, as it also taught me about communication and how to explain my business idea and prompt clearly and specifically. Trying to ensure that the platform made sense was a bit more difficult, as I had to think from the perspective of a property owner and an establishment interested in renting. I needed it to make sense for both sides, but it was hard to imagine it from each perspective, as my point of view is the entire bigger picture and operation. 
3. If a competitor had the same AI tool, how long would it take them to copy your site?
It would take a competitor a very short amount of time to copy this site. Depending on the resources they have and how they are creating it, it could take anywhere from 3 hours to 2 weeks. The website and idea itself is not a competitive advantage or something special by any means, as anyone could do that. This is definitely concerning and puts into perspective how much the business and entrepreneurial world is (or should be) shifting because of AI platforms. As a business owner today, there would be so many more worries about competitors and imitation than there would have been when AI was not as accessible to society.
